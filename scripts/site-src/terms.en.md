## 5. What you can expect from a design
Designs are AI-generated ideas to inspire you. They are not exact products, measurements, building plans or professional advice.

- We do not guarantee a specific result, or that a design will match the style exactly, keep every detail of your room, or please you. A credit is used when a design is delivered, whether or not you like it.
- Furniture and objects in a design may not exist as real products.
- Check sizes, structure, wiring and safety with a qualified person before you buy or build anything based on a design.
- In Christmas mode the tree in a design is an illustration. A real tree can differ in size, shape and colour, so measure your space before you order one.

**Shop links.** In Christmas mode DecoHero shows links to stores. Some are affiliate links, marked in the app: if you buy after tapping one, we may earn a commission. The store is a separate business. Its prices, delivery, returns and terms are its own, and anything you buy there is between you and the store.
