## What we collect
- **Room photos you choose to upload.** Sent to our server and to Google's Gemini API only to create your designs. We do not store your photos or the generated designs on our servers after the request completes. Google processes them under the Gemini API terms.
- **An anonymous install ID** created on your device, used to keep track of your credit balance. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
- **Purchase records** (product, date, install ID) processed by Google Play and RevenueCat to deliver credits.
- **IP address**, used temporarily for rate limiting and fraud prevention.
- **Reports** you submit about a design (which style, when).

## What we don't do
We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your photos to train AI models.

## Permissions
Camera: to photograph a room. Photos/media: to save designs you choose to your gallery.

## Retention and deletion
Credit and purchase records are kept while you use the app. To delete them, email [EMAIL] with the install ID shown in [Settings, if you add it] or the purchase date and we'll erase them within 30 days.

## Children
