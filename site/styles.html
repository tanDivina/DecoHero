Interior design styles

12 interior styles on the same rooms

Style names are easier to tell apart when the room stays the same. Each style below was applied by DecoHero to the same apartment living room, bedroom and Panama living room, keeping the walls and windows. Open a style to see all its rooms and what makes the look.

  • Tropical modern

    Teak and rattan, white plaster, large tropical plants, linen.

  • Minimal

    Clean lines, warm whites and greys, hidden storage, one statement light.

  • Japandi

    Low natural-wood furniture, muted earthy tones, paper lamps, ceramics.

  • Scandinavian

    Light oak, white walls, wool and linen, soft greys, plants.

  • Mid-century

    Walnut furniture on tapered legs, mustard and teal accents, globe lights.

  • Boho

    Layered rugs, macrame, rattan, terracotta pots, many plants.

  • Coastal

    Whites and soft blues, driftwood, linen slipcovers, a jute rug.

  • Mediterranean

    Limewashed walls, terracotta tones, wrought iron, olive wood.

  • Industrial

    Black metal, reclaimed wood, leather, exposed bulbs, concrete tones.

  • Classic luxury

    Velvet upholstery, marble, brass details, a symmetrical layout.

  • Farmhouse

    Distressed wood, black hardware, neutral linens, vintage accents.

  • Art deco

    Geometric patterns, emerald and gold, curved velvet furniture, lacquer.

How to pick a style

Start with what you can't change: the floor, the windows, the climate and how much daylight the room gets. Tile floors and strong sun lean toward tropical modern, coastal or Mediterranean. Small, dim rooms lean toward Scandinavian or Japandi. Then look at the one or two pieces you're keeping, such as a leather sofa or a walnut sideboard, and pick the style they already belong to.
