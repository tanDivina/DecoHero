AI room design

Redesign your room from a photo

Take a photo of a room, pick up to four interior styles, and DecoHero returns a photoreal redesign of that same room for each style. The walls, windows, doors and camera angle stay where they are; the furniture, colours, materials and lighting change.

Before and after, on real sample rooms

Every redesign below is the app's real output for the "before" photo next to it, made with the same instructions the app uses. Only rejected first tries were thrown away, when the AI changed the room itself.

Sample rooms. Every room here is AI-generated, so no photo shows a real person's home. Designs are ideas, not exact products or measurements.

How it works

  1. Take a photo of the room or pick one from your gallery.
  2. Choose up to four of the 12 styles. Each style uses one credit and gives one design.
  3. Designs arrive one at a time. Press and hold a design to flip back to your original photo, and save the ones you like.

There is no prompt to write. The app sends your photo with fixed instructions to Google's Gemini image model, which tell it to keep the architecture and change the furnishing.

Photos that work best

  • Stand in a corner and hold the phone level, so most of the room is in frame.
  • Shoot in daylight with the lights on. Dark photos give dark, muddy designs.
  • Clear the floor of bags and laundry. The AI keeps what it sees as part of the room.
  • Landscape photos suit wide rooms, portrait photos suit narrow ones.

What it gets wrong

The AI sometimes changes the room itself. While making the samples on this page, first tries added ceiling beams, moved a window, added a door that wasn't there, or swapped barred windows for plain glass. When that happens, run the style again. A design that fails outright gives its credit back; a design you just don't like does not. Furniture in the designs is an idea, not a product you can order, and sizes are not measured.

What it costs

Your first design is free when you sign in. After that, credits come in packs: 15 designs for $3.99, 50 for $9.99 and 150 for $24.99. There is no subscription and credits don't expire. It works on Android from Google Play and in any browser, iPhone included, at deco.hero-apps.com/app.

Questions

Common questions

Is there a free AI room design app?

DecoHero gives you one free design when you sign in. More designs are paid, from $3.99 for 15. There is no subscription.

Does the AI keep my room's layout?

It is instructed to keep the walls, windows, doors, floor plan and camera angle and to change only furniture, colours, materials and lighting. It sometimes changes the room anyway; run the style again when it does.

What happens to my photo?

It goes to DecoHero's server, which sends it to Google's paid Gemini API and returns the design. The server handles photos in memory and doesn't store them. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it work on iPhone?

There is no iPhone app yet. On an iPhone, the web app at deco.hero-apps.com/app runs in the browser.
