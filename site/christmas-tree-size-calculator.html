8 foot ceiling: a 7 foot tree

Eight feet is the standard ceiling in most US homes and apartments, and the tallest tree for it is 7 ft. The popular 7.5 ft artificial tree leaves only 6 inches, which is tight once the stand lifts the tree and the topper goes on. If you love a 7.5 ft tree, pair it with a low stand and a flat star, or choose 7 ft and keep the tall topper.

9 foot ceiling: an 8 foot tree

A 9 ft ceiling takes an 8 ft tree. If your topper is tall, like a large star or a big bow, drop to 7.5 ft so it does not bend against the ceiling.

10 foot ceiling and higher

For 10 ft, buy a 9 ft tree. Above that the same rule holds, but very tall trees get wide: a full 9 ft fir can be about 6 ft across at the bottom, so check the floor before you check the ceiling. With a vaulted or sloped ceiling, measure at the exact spot where the tree will stand, not at the highest point of the room.

Sold as "6-7 ft"? Use the top number

Real trees are cut by hand and often sold in ranges, and a tree listed as 6-7 ft can arrive at 7 ft. Plan for the top of the range. The farm-cut trees DecoHero links to are sold this way, so the app picks the tree by the tallest size that may arrive.

Check the width, too

A 7 ft fir that is about 4.5 to 5 ft wide at the bottom needs a circle of floor that size, plus room to walk past it. Before you order, put a strip of tape on the floor where the lowest branches will reach. If the circle blocks a door or a walkway, go one size down or choose a slimmer tree.

How to measure your ceiling

Measure from the floor to the ceiling at the spot where the tree will stand, with a tape measure or a laser measure. Older homes and rooms with beams or a sloped ceiling are often lower in one corner than in the middle of the room.

Questions

Common questions

What size Christmas tree for an 8 foot ceiling?

A 7 ft tree. That leaves 12 inches for the stand and the topper.

Will a 7.5 ft tree fit under an 8 ft ceiling?

Only just. It leaves 6 inches, so you need a low stand and a flat topper. A 7 ft tree is the safer choice.

What size Christmas tree for a 9 foot ceiling?

An 8 ft tree, or 7.5 ft if your topper is tall.

What size Christmas tree for a 10 foot ceiling?

A 9 ft tree. Check the width as well: a full 9 ft fir can be about 6 ft across at the bottom.

How wide is a 7 ft Christmas tree?

A full farm-grown fir is usually about two thirds as wide as it is tall, so about 4.5 to 5 ft at the bottom. Slim and pencil trees are much narrower.

What size tree for a small apartment?

Measure the ceiling and the floor space. A 5 or 6 ft tree suits most apartment living rooms. With very little floor, a 3 or 4 ft tree on a side table works.
