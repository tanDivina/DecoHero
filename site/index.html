How it works

Three steps from photo to redesign.

  1. 01

    Photograph the room

    Take a photo or choose one you already have. Stand in a corner and shoot in daylight so the whole room is in frame.

  2. 02

    Pick up to four styles

    Choose from 12 interior styles. Each style you pick uses one credit and gives you one design.

  3. 03

    Compare and keep

    Designs arrive one at a time. Press and hold a design to flip back to your original room, then save the ones you like.

Questions

Before you upload a photo

What happens to my photos?

Your photo goes to our server, which passes it to Google's Gemini API to create the design and sends the result back to you. Our server handles the photo and the design in memory only. It doesn't write them to a database, to disk or to logs.

We use Google's paid Gemini API, so Google doesn't use your photos to improve its products, though it may keep them for a limited time to detect abuse. We don't use your photos to train AI models. The privacy policy has the details.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits you buy stay on your account until you use them. Deleting your account is the one exception: unused credits are lost.

Can I get a refund?

When a design fails, the credit returns to your balance on its own. For a refund of a purchase, email support@hero-apps.com from the address you sign in with. A refunded pack's credits are removed from your balance. See the terms.

Is there an Android app? An iPhone app?

Yes, DecoHero is on Google Play. There is no iPhone app yet. On an iPhone, the web app runs in your browser.

Do I need an account?

On the web, yes. You sign in with Google so your credits stay with you. Our server stores an ID for your sign-in, not your email address, name or profile picture. You can delete the account from the app's About screen.

How close are the designs to reality?

They are AI-generated ideas, not exact products or measurements. DecoHero is built to keep the room's architecture and change the furniture, colours, materials and lighting. Results depend on the photo, and a daylight shot from a corner works best.

How does the Christmas tree work?

In Christmas mode DecoHero adds one tree to your photo and keeps the rest of the room in place. Enter your ceiling height and the tallest tree that fits is selected, leaving at least 12 in (30 cm) under the ceiling for the stand and the topper. You can pick a smaller size. Tap the photo where the tree should go, or let DecoHero choose. The tree stands on the open floor nearest your tap.

With your results you get links to buy a real farm-cut tree from our partner's store, which delivers in the US only. Some of these are affiliate links, so we may earn a commission if you buy through them. The picture is an estimate: measure your ceiling before you order.
