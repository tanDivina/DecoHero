Christmas tree themes

Six Christmas tree themes, and what goes on each

A theme is a short list: a few ornament colours, one kind of light, a topper and a skirt. These are the six themes in DecoHero's Christmas mode, with the exact parts of each. Three are shown on the same sample apartment so you can compare them.

Sample room. Designs are AI-generated ideas, not exact products or measurements.

Classic warmth

Warm amber-white lights and glossy glass baubles in deep red and gold, spaced evenly. A many-pointed Moravian star on top, a deep red velvet skirt, and gifts in red, gold and cream paper. It suits traditional rooms with wood furniture and warm lamps.

Winter wonderland

A tree flocked white so the branches look covered in snow, with tiny warm white fairy lights, silver crystal ornaments, iced snowflakes and mercury-glass baubles in silver and champagne. A silver snowflake on top and a white faux fur skirt. Note that flocking is a finish: a farm-cut tree arrives green, so you add the flocking yourself or buy a pre-flocked artificial tree.

Nordic minimal

A full green fir with only a little on it: warm white micro lights, a small number of raw wooden ornaments and a plain wooden star, so plenty of green shows. No tinsel and nothing shiny. A seagrass collar instead of a skirt and two gifts in white paper with twine. It works in Scandinavian, Japandi and minimal rooms.

Pink coquette

Blush pink and pearl white: velvet bows clipped to the branches, pearl baubles, a few small mirrored disco-ball ornaments, warm fairy lights and a large blush velvet bow on top. A white faux fur skirt and gifts in pink and white with satin ribbon. Keep the pink to one or two shades so it stays soft.

Heritage tartan

A dark green tree with wide tartan ribbon woven through the branches, in navy-and-green Black Watch and in red tartan, plus brass bells and a few deep red and antique gold baubles. A tartan bow on top, a woven rattan collar and gifts in kraft paper with tartan ribbon. It fits farmhouse and classic rooms, and rooms with a fireplace.

Tropical Christmas

For a home in a hot climate: warm white lights, ornaments in natural wood and woven rattan, seashells and soft coral baubles, a woven rattan star and a seagrass basket collar. Gifts in kraft paper with green leaves under the ribbon. Nothing snowy. It pairs well with tropical modern, coastal and boho rooms.

How to choose a theme for your room

Start from what is already in the room. Pick ornament colours that repeat one colour you already have, such as the wood of the floor, a cushion or a rug, and match the lights to your lamps: warm white for most rooms, cooler white only with cool, bright interiors. If you can't decide, try two or three on a photo of your room and compare them side by side.

Questions

Common questions

What are popular Christmas tree themes?

Classic red and gold, white winter wonderland, Nordic minimal with wood ornaments, pink coquette with bows, tartan with brass bells, and natural tropical themes with rattan and shells.

How many colours should a Christmas tree theme have?

Two or three, plus the light colour. Fewer colours look calmer and make a theme easier to recognise.

What Christmas tree theme suits a small room?

Light themes with fewer ornaments, such as Nordic minimal, keep a small room from looking crowded. A seagrass collar takes less floor than a wide skirt.
