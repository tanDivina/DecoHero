Boho style

Boho living room and bedroom ideas

Boho is layered and relaxed: rugs on top of rugs, macrame, rattan, terracotta pots, many plants and warm patterned textiles. It looks collected over years, with pieces from different places that share warm colours.

Sample rooms, all AI-generated: no photo shows a real person's home. Designs are ideas, not exact products or measurements.

What makes a room Boho

  • Layered rugs with patterns
  • Macrame wall hangings
  • Rattan and cane furniture
  • Terracotta pots and many plants
  • Warm patterned cushions and throws
  • Floor cushions and low seating

Four changes to start with

  1. Layer a smaller patterned rug over a plain jute one.
  2. Add three or more plants at different heights.
  3. Mix cushions in two or three patterns that share one warm colour.
  4. Hang a macrame or woven piece where a print used to be.

Where it works

It suits rental rooms because almost all of it is textiles and plants. In a small room, keep the colours warm and the patterns few, or it looks busy.
