Classic luxury style

Classic luxury living room and bedroom ideas

Classic luxury is formal and balanced: velvet upholstery, marble, brass details, a chandelier and a symmetrical layout, with matching pieces on either side of a centre point.

Sample rooms, all AI-generated: no photo shows a real person's home. Designs are ideas, not exact products or measurements.

What makes a room Classic luxury

  • Velvet sofas and chairs
  • Marble on tables or the fireplace
  • Brass lamps, handles and frames
  • A chandelier
  • A symmetrical layout around a centre point
  • Pairs: two lamps, two chairs, two side tables

Four changes to start with

  1. Arrange the room around one centre point, such as the sofa or the bed, with pairs on either side.
  2. Add matching table lamps in brass.
  3. Swap a pendant for a small chandelier.
  4. Bring in velvet through cushions or one chair.

Where it works

It suits rooms with high ceilings and mouldings, and formal living and dining rooms. In a small room, use one chandelier and fewer pieces, all in pairs.
