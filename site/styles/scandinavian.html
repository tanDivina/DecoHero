Scandinavian style

Scandinavian living room and bedroom ideas

Scandinavian rooms are light and cosy at the same time: light oak, white walls, wool and linen, soft greys and green plants. The style comes from long dark winters, so it chases daylight and adds warmth with textiles.

Sample rooms, all AI-generated: no photo shows a real person's home. Designs are ideas, not exact products or measurements.

What makes a room scandinavian

  • White walls and light oak floors or furniture
  • Soft greys, off-whites and pale blue
  • Wool throws, sheepskins and linen
  • Simple wooden chairs and tables
  • Plants in plain pots
  • Several small lamps instead of one bright light

Four changes to start with

  1. Paint the walls white or a warm off-white.
  2. Add a wool throw and a sheepskin to the sofa.
  3. Put two or three small lamps around the room for evenings.
  4. Choose light wood for the next piece you buy.

Where it works

It suits rooms with little daylight and small rooms that should feel larger. In a very bright, hot room, tropical modern or coastal often fits better.
