Mediterranean style

Mediterranean living room and bedroom ideas

Mediterranean rooms borrow from houses in southern Spain, Italy and Greece: limewashed walls, terracotta tones, wrought iron, olive wood and touches of blue. The walls do a lot of the work, with texture and soft, uneven colour.

Sample rooms, all AI-generated: no photo shows a real person's home. Designs are ideas, not exact products or measurements.

What makes a room Mediterranean

  • Limewashed or textured plaster walls
  • Terracotta floors or terracotta tones
  • Wrought iron in lights, mirrors and chairs
  • Olive wood and rustic wood beams
  • Blue accents in ceramics or textiles
  • Arches, where the room already has them

Four changes to start with

  1. Limewash one wall; it changes the room more than any furniture.
  2. Add a wrought iron light or mirror.
  3. Bring in terracotta through pots, tiles or a rug.
  4. Use blue in ceramics, not on the walls.

Where it works

It suits warm climates, tiled floors and homes with arches or thick walls. In a small modern apartment, keep it light: limewash and ceramics, without heavy dark wood.
