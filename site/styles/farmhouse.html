Farmhouse style

Modern farmhouse living room and bedroom ideas

Modern farmhouse mixes country materials with clean, neutral rooms: distressed wood, black hardware, neutral linens and vintage accents. It is warm and practical rather than rustic.

Sample rooms, all AI-generated: no photo shows a real person's home. Designs are ideas, not exact products or measurements.

What makes a room farmhouse

  • Distressed or reclaimed wood
  • Black hardware, lights and window frames
  • Neutral linens in white, cream and grey
  • Vintage accents: baskets, crocks, old frames
  • Shiplap or board-and-batten walls
  • Slipcovered sofas

Four changes to start with

  1. Swap handles and light fixtures for black.
  2. Add a distressed wood piece: a coffee table, a bench or a shelf.
  3. Use white and cream linen for the sofa, curtains and bedding.
  4. Hang a few vintage frames or baskets.

Where it works

It suits kitchens, family rooms and houses with wood floors. In a sleek modern apartment it can look like a costume; pick two or three elements instead of all of them.
